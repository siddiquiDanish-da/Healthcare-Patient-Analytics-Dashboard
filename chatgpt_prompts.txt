PROMPT 1 — MONTHLY HOSPITAL PERFORMANCE SUMMARY REPORT
================================================================
Use this prompt every month after running your SQL queries.
Paste your actual KPI numbers in the brackets.

PROMPT:
"You are a professional healthcare data analyst. Based on the
following hospital KPIs for [Month] [Year], write a formal
1-page Monthly Hospital Performance Summary Report.

KPI Data:
- Total Patient Admissions : [paste number]
- Readmission Rate         : [paste number]%
- Average Treatment Cost   : Rs. [paste number]
- Average Length of Stay   : [paste number] days
- Patient Recovery Rate    : [paste number]%
- Cost Per Patient         : Rs. [paste number]
- Top Department           : [paste department name]
- Top Diagnosis            : [paste diagnosis name]
- Bed Occupancy Rate       : [paste number]%

The report should include:
1. Executive Summary (2-3 lines)
2. Key Highlights (bullet points)
3. Areas of Concern (any KPI that is worse than last month)
4. Recommendations (3 actionable points)
5. Conclusion (1-2 lines)

Format it professionally. Use clear headings."

----------------------------------------------------------------
EXAMPLE OUTPUT THIS PROMPT GIVES:
Monthly Hospital Performance Summary — January 2024

Executive Summary:
The hospital recorded 265 admissions in January 2024 with an
average treatment cost of Rs.1,04,540. Recovery rate stood at
70.14% with a readmission rate of 15.63%.

Key Highlights:
• General Medicine had the highest patient volume
• Recovery rate improved by 2% vs last month
• Average length of stay remained stable at 15 days

Areas of Concern:
• Readmission rate of 15.63% is above the 10% benchmark
• No-insurance patients paying 3x more out-of-pocket

Recommendations:
1. Implement 30-day post-discharge follow-up for Cardiology
2. Expand government scheme outreach for uninsured patients
3. Review bed allocation in high-volume departments
